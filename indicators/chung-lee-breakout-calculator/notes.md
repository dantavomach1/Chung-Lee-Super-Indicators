# Notes

## Purpose

`Chung Lee Breakout Calculator` is the repository name for the supplied Pine Script v6 indicator. Its preserved Pine declaration uses the title `Breakout Calculator` and short title `BO Calc`.

## Current Behavior

Compares each studied bar's high and low with the prior bar, classifies higher-high and lower-low breakouts, no-breakout bars, one-sided breakouts, and two-sided breakouts, then summarizes counts, percentages, average and maximum distances, and modes in tables.

## Inputs

Includes point size, statistics time zone, a lookback of up to 4,999 bars, active-bar exclusion, and visibility switches for all-bars and selected hourly tables at 3pm, 7pm, 11pm, 3am, 7am, and 11am, plus a combined 11pm / 3am table.

## Visuals

Uses eight on-chart tables for the enabled statistical views.

The supplied source contains no alert declarations.
